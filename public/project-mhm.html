Our Projects

Menstrual Hygiene Management

Highlights

  1. CACR is at the forefront of creating awareness about Menstrual Hygiene in schools of Mumbai.
  2. Its primary goal is to break myths and taboos regarding menstruation.
  3. This program has resulted in decreased absenteeism and dropouts due to menstruation.
  4. It imparts scientific knowledge regarding menstruation among children.
  5. Additionally, it improves the hygiene habits of adolescent girls.

Menstruation is not a problem,
poor menstrual hygiene is.

The Company

CACR - Cititzens Action for Child Rights is a network of like minded citizens concerned about ensuring child rights with specific focus on ensuring good quality of education and health for all children.

We are a Section 25 company.
